# Glossary

A reference for the terminology used across the QuantumEd curriculum. It answers one question, *"What does this term mean?"*, and points you to the learning modules when you want to know how a concept works and why it matters.

Each entry has:

- a **definition**: a concise, technically accurate statement of the term;
- a **beginner interpretation**: the same idea in plainer language, meant to build intuition, not replace the definition;
- **related terms**: neighboring concepts worth reading next; and
- **appears in**: the lessons where the term is taught or used (this list grows as modules are added).

Abbreviations and alternate names are listed on the entry they belong to, so searching for "QEC" finds *Quantum Error Correction*. A few entries describe active research areas; those are worded to avoid implying that any one approach is standard.
